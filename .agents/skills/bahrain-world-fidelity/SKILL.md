## Fidelity rules

1. Preserve recognizable coastline, district relationships, road direction, and landmark placement at the intended compressed scale.
2. Document every meaningful geographic deviation and never call a compressed map literal 1:1 Bahrain.
3. Prioritize Manama waterfront, Bab Al Bahrain, Manama Souq, Bahrain World Trade Center, Bahrain Financial Harbour, King Faisal Highway, Bahrain Fort, and traditional dhow areas.
4. Use Arabic and English signage accurately, with readable hierarchy and culturally appropriate naming.
5. Avoid political, religious, or cultural caricature and avoid precise real-world player geolocation.
6. Use original landmark interpretations rather than copied commercial models.

## Review output

- Source evidence and date.
- Recognizable relationships preserved.
- Compression and deviation ledger.
- Cultural and language findings.
- Gameplay readability and mobile-streaming implications.
- Required asset and provenance updates.
